AgentPay Guard

Register your Agent ID identity, set an on-chain spending policy (per-transaction cap, daily cap, recipient allowlist, human-confirm threshold), and let your agent pay only within those rules. Payments above your confirm threshold need your explicit on-chain approval first — one approval covers exactly one payment.

Risk note. This is a policy-enforcement tool for your own funds, not an investment product. It offers no returns, yield, or profit of any kind. Funds you deposit are held by a smart contract; smart contracts carry risk, including the risk of bugs. Deposit only what you are prepared to manage carefully. You can withdraw your own funds at any time. Never enter a seed phrase or private key anywhere in this app — it will never ask for one.
